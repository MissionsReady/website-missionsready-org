# Logo Specifications

The MissionsReady logo uses the following exact geometry:

- Geometric shield shape.
- Centered white cross.
- Soft gold stroke (`#D9A441`).
- Deep blue fill (`#1E3A5F`).
- 20% safe zone padding.
- Full-color and monochrome variants.

Do not stretch, rotate, crop, redraw, recolor, or place the logo on a background that reduces its legibility.
