# Our Mission

MissionsReady equips ministries with secure, dependable technology that is practical to adopt and simple to operate.

## Reproducible Infrastructure

Reproducible infrastructure can be rebuilt from documented inputs and produce the same expected result. For ministries, this means technology is easier to understand, recover, maintain, and hand over when staff or volunteers change.

MissionsReady combines secure defaults, repeatable blueprints, documented deployments, and transparent security practices so ministries can focus more energy on serving people.
