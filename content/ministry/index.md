# For Ministries

MissionsReady helps ministries adopt useful technology without requiring every staff member or volunteer to become a systems expert.

## Adoption Steps

1. Identify the ministry need and choose a suitable application.
2. Review the application blueprint and deployment requirements.
3. Assign a primary owner and a backup contact.
4. Configure a domain, secure access, and encrypted connections.
5. Create individual accounts with only the access each person needs.
6. Configure and test encrypted backups before entering important data.
7. Train a small group of users and record operational questions.
8. Schedule regular updates, access reviews, and recovery tests.

## Non-Technical Guidance

Start with the ministry's work rather than the technology. Define who needs the service, what information it will contain, and who will be responsible for it. Keep shared passwords out of email and chat, use multi-factor authentication when available, and ask the technical owner to explain any unfamiliar setting before changing it.

If a service stops working, record what happened and when, then contact the designated technical owner. Do not delete data or make additional changes while recovery is being assessed.
