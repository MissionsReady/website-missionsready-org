# Ministry-Ready Applications

MissionsReady supports applications that help ministries manage people, communication, worship, media, and day-to-day operations. Applications are selected and documented with secure deployment, maintainability, and ministry usability in mind.

## Applications

- [ChurchCRM](https://github.com/MissionsReady/applications-churchcrm) - Manage church contacts, groups, volunteers, and ministry relationships.
- [Kardia](https://github.com/MissionsReady/applications-kardia) - Support ministry operations with an application designed for dependable service.
- [FreeShow](https://github.com/MissionsReady/applications-freeshow) - Prepare and present worship and ministry media.
- [OpenLP](https://github.com/MissionsReady/applications-openlp) - Manage and display songs, scripture, presentations, and service media.

Each application should be deployed from documented guidance, use individual accounts, maintain tested backups, and follow its published update process.
